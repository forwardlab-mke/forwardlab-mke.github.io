Lab news

Updates from the ForWARD Lab, newest first.

  1. Fall 2026

    The ForWARD Lab opens at the Medical College of Wisconsin

    Jamie Hanson joins the Department of Pediatrics at MCW and Children's Wisconsin, and the ForWARD Lab begins its work in Milwaukee.

  2. Summer 2026

    Isabella Kahhalé wins the APA Division 12 Dissertation Award

    The Society of Clinical Psychology recognized Isabella's dissertation. Isabella also received the Division's Distinguished Student Practice Award in 2025.

  3. Winter 2026

    New paper: positive affect, early adversity, and internalizing symptoms

    Published in the Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, with D. J. Adkins, Isabella Kahhalé, and Sriparna Sen. See selected papers.

  4. Summer 2025

    NIMH funds a study of early adversity and affective dynamics

    A five-year R01 (MH136109) combines daily smartphone check-ins with brain imaging to understand depression risk. More on our funding.
