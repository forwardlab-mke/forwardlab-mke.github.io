Join us

Join TaLE MKE

We are building the lab in Milwaukee and welcome inquiries from people at every stage. There are no specific postings right now, but we are always glad to hear from people whose interests fit ours.

  • Graduate students

    Students interested in developmental science, neuroscience, and health who want to work with kids, families, and longitudinal data.

  • Postdoctoral fellows

    Researchers with interests in early experience, brain development, aging, or open and reproducible methods.

  • Research staff and students

    Research coordinators, research assistants, and undergraduate researchers in the Milwaukee area.

To get in touch, email Jamie at jamhanson@mcw.edu with a CV and a few sentences about your interests.
